1v0.io Privacy Policy

For the 1v0.io app on iPhone and iPad and the web version. Effective November 15, 2026.

The short version

1v0.io does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no server. Your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress, Bolts, cosmetics, settings, saved lobbies and the player name you choose are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage (in the web version, in your browser's storage). They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes them, and Settings > Data > Reset all progress wipes them at any time.

Playing online (optional)

Everything works offline against bots. If you make or join a party, or press Quick Match, the game connects you to the other players like this:

  • PeerJS, a free public service that the developer does not run, introduces the devices to each other. It sees your device's IP address and a random connection id for as long as you are connected.
  • A Google STUN server tells your device its own public address so the other players can reach it. When a direct connection isn't possible, PeerJS's relay server passes the game traffic along. That traffic is encrypted.
  • After that, the game talks directly to the other players' devices over an encrypted WebRTC connection.

The other players in your match see the name you chose, your character and its looks, and what you do in the match. Because the connection is direct, they could also find out your IP address (the Quick Match screen says so). Don't use your real name as your player name.

Chat

Typed chat only works between friends in a private party, who join with a code you share. It is filtered: swear words and slurs are hidden, and links, email addresses, social media handles and phone numbers are blanked. You can turn typed chat off in Settings > Accessibility > Free chat. Players you meet through Quick Match can only use the preset quick-chat phrases.

You can mute any player from the pause menu, which hides everything they say. The host of a party or a Quick Match room can remove a player before a match starts. Chat messages are not stored anywhere.

Children

1v0.io does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is sent to the developer.

The web version

The web version (https://l3dprints26.github.io/1v0io/) is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so those services receive your IP address like any website you visit. A copy of the game downloaded as a file checks the website for a newer version. The App Store app does neither: its fonts are built in and the App Store handles updates.
